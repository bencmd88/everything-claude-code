# DOOR CLOSER COMPARISON — PRIMARY SCHOOL APPLICATION
**Aluminium Double-Glazed Doors | High-Wind Exposure | DDA Compliant Accessways**
NCC 2022 | AS1428.1:2009 | DDA 1992

---

**MANDATORY DESIGN REQUIREMENTS — This Application**

- ≤ 20N maximum opening force — DDA / NCC 2022 Part D4 / AS1428.1:2009
- Hold-open or delayed-action function required — NCC 2022
- Doorstop to restrict end travel — VBA Design Guidance
- EN1154 compliant closer — AS1905.1 fire door requirement

---

|---|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | CAM Action — force profile engineered for DDA compliance | Rack & Pinion — constant mechanical force regardless of arc position |
